# Pre-application package

Create this package only for shortlisted candidates selected by the user or clearly identified as top candidates. Research each recipient before drafting.

## Required outputs

### Outreach drafts

For each selected candidate, write a concise draft that:

- uses the recipient's verified name and title;
- identifies one or two specific, current research connections with citations in the accompanying notes, not fabricated praise;
- links the applicant's most relevant evidence to a plausible contribution;
- states the intended opportunity and cycle;
- asks one clear, appropriate question;
- avoids attachments or claims the applicant has not approved.

Choose the recipient language from official communication norms and the user's preference. When uncertain, default to professional English. Never send automatically.

### CV suggestions

Give candidate-specific changes as recommendations, not fabricated entries. Map existing evidence to the target group's themes, methods, and opportunity requirements. Preserve dates, authorship, metrics, and role descriptions exactly unless the user supplies corrections.

### Research-plan entry points

Suggest two or three defensible project intersections for each top candidate. Separate:

- what follows from the candidate's published direction;
- what follows from the applicant's experience;
- what is a proposed bridge or hypothesis.

Do not attribute an unannounced project, grant, dataset, or opening to the group.

### Statement evidence map

Create a table linking each application claim to applicant evidence and target evidence. Flag unsupported claims for removal or further substantiation.

## Application route and funding

Include official program steps, supervisor-contact rules, deadlines, internal funding, external scholarships, national schemes, and role-specific requirements. Label funding as guaranteed, competitive, possible, or unknown. Use current official sources.

## Interview gate

Do not generate interview questions, interview strategy, or mock-interview materials during ordinary search or pre-application work. Generate them only after the user says an interview has been obtained or explicitly requests interview preparation.

## Boundaries

- Never send messages or submit applications without explicit authorization for that action.
- Never invent personal familiarity, paper readership, results, credentials, or availability.
- Do not recommend mass identical outreach.
- Do not expose private applicant information beyond what is necessary for the draft.
