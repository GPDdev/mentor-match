## Short description

Evidence-backed matching for research supervisors, labs, programs, and funding.

## Long description

Mentor Match helps applicants worldwide turn a resume and application goals into a current, evidence-backed shortlist of research supervisors, laboratories, postdoctoral hosts, research-assistant opportunities, programs, and funding routes. It searches live public sources, respects institution and department boundaries, explains adaptive ranking weights, distinguishes confirmed facts from inference, and exports Markdown, CSV, and JSON. It can also prepare reviewed outreach drafts and refresh saved shortlists. It never guarantees admission or sends applications automatically.

## Starter prompts

1. Match my CV with PhD supervisors in computational biology and explain every score.
2. Search these universities for robotics labs, restricted to the named departments.
3. Find funded postdoctoral hosts in protein design across universities, nonprofit institutes, and industry research labs.
4. Audit my saved shortlist for changed affiliations, openings, deadlines, and funding.
5. Prepare a pre-application pack for my top three candidates, but do not send anything.

## Availability proposal

Global availability, subject to publisher support readiness and the country selections available in the submission portal. The Skill follows the user's language while preserving official names and citations in their source language.

## Data and capability disclosure

Mentor Match browses public web sources through the host Agent. After one-time informed consent, it may store resumes, preferences, reports, and history as local plain files under `~/.mentor-match/` or a user-selected path. It has no bundled remote database, server, analytics, advertising, or telemetry. Scheduling is optional and only used when the host provides an authorized scheduler.
