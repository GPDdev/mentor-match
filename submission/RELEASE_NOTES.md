# Mentor Match 1.0.0

Initial public release candidate.

- Adds live, evidence-backed matching for master's, PhD, postdoctoral, and research-assistant searches.
- Supports universities, public or nonprofit research institutes, and industry research labs worldwide.
- Adds bounded institution, school, and department searches with quick, standard, deep, and refresh workflows.
- Adds adaptive, disclosed scoring with source confidence and a 10% maximum for advising-culture signals.
- Exports Markdown, CSV, JSON, and search logs.
- Adds consent-gated local profiles, application cases, immutable run histories, export, archive, and deletion controls.
- Adds tailored pre-application packs; interview preparation remains opt-in after an interview is obtained or explicitly requested.
- Adds meaningful-change monitoring instructions with a manual fallback when scheduling is unavailable.
- Includes privacy, terms, security, deterministic tests, and behavioral review cases.

Known boundary: the package does not send messages, submit applications, guarantee recruitment, or maintain a bundled supervisor database.
